02Quantitative Research2026

S&P 500 market regimes & sector rotation↗

Problem
Does sector performance depend on market conditions — and can you rotate into the right sectors using only what you'd know at the time?
My role
Built it end to end on 25 years of S&P 500 data: the feature pipeline, regime classification, sector ranking within each regime, and a backtest that only uses data available at each rebalance date.
Result
Beat the equal-weight benchmark on total return, but not on a risk-adjusted basis — a slightly lower Sharpe, a deeper drawdown, and behind in each stress window tested. The extra return came from extra risk.
2.9M+daily price observations engineered
15+technical features per stock
9market regimes — trend × volatility
437% vs 398%full-period cumulative return, strategy vs equal-weight benchmark
0.96 vs 0.98Sharpe ratio — the benchmark was slightly better risk-adjusted

03Applied ML Research2025 — 2026

Oncology clinical decision support↗

Problem
Can a small model, fine-tuned, beat its own base and GPT-3.5 on structured clinical tasks?
My role
Fine-tuned Med42-8B on 14k synthetic NSCLC cases with QLoRA across six runs. When 30% of one run's outputs were duplicates, I rewrote the data generator and regenerated the corpus. I built evaluation beyond ROUGE, since ROUGE scored a wrong drug recommendation at 97.8.
Result
2.8× the base model and GPT-3.5 on ROUGE-1, and the only model that held its format across all three task types.
0.705ROUGE-1 on held-out test data — 2.8× both base Med42 and GPT-3.5
71.4%clinically appropriate drug recommendations on the held-out drug-recall cases
3.80/4format adherence — the only model that holds structure across all three task types
30% → 0.25%duplicate response rate after regenerating the 14k-case corpus
Stack

Python · PyTorch · QLoRA · Hugging Face · Hex · SQL
